rule must-consume
reason bench/ | the benches hold the endpoints of a channel and a token for the run of a bench, and drop them at the end
reason include/crucible/ | the runtime mints a gate token that ends at the end of its scope, and it reads a linear handle in std::expected through an accessor
reason include/fixy/ | the wrappers move a token out of a field of a marked class or out of a tuple through std::get, and consume an upgrade behind a test of its option

reason src/ | each stage of the background pipeline drops its endpoint of a channel at the end of its function, and the start drops one producer
reason test/ | the tests drop tokens and handles at the end of a test, consume a value two times to show the abort, and discard linear temporaries.  Some tests consume a token behind a test of its option
