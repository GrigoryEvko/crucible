# The ledger of the rule escape-doors: the doors and the public data members
# of the surface of the base headers that hand out a raw reference or
# pointer, with the reason of each.  `python3 utils/bin/guard explain
# escape-doors` tells the rule.
#
# One row is `PATH | KIND | admit | ENTITY | REASON`.  The kind is bare-door,
# hatch, public-member or unread-surface.  The entity of a door is the full
# name of the function with the types of its parameters and its qualifiers.
# The entity of a data member is its full name, and CLASS via MEMBER when a
# base or a using-declaration gives it.  Each hatch has its own row, and a new
# hatch is then a reviewed act.  A row that admits no finding passes, and a
# write removes it.
