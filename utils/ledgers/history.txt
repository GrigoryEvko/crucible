# The ledger of the rule history: the rises of the past that no measurement in a commit admits, the
# commits that changed the key or the format of a ledger or made the scope of a rule larger, the
# growth of a ledger that is wanted, and the thresholds that a measurement keeps above their lowest
# values.  A row PATH | KIND | admit | ENTITY | REASON admits one error of the rule.  The row
# utils/registry.txt | warn|error | admit | COMMIT RULE | REASON admits the rise of a threshold, and
# the row utils/ledgers/quarantine.txt | raise | admit | COMMIT | REASON admits a raise of the
# quarantine ledger.  The row utils/ledgers/quarantine.txt | region-reason | admit | COMMIT | REASON
# admits a raised count of opted-out or region whose commit does not give the reason of its region.
# REASON gives the measurement or the reason that the commit did not give.  The row
# utils/ledgers/RULE.txt | rebase | admit | COMMIT | REASON starts the lowest count of a ledger again
# at a commit that changed its key or its format.  At a commit that made the scope of the rule larger,
# the row does not count as growth the rows that the commit added in files that it did not change.
# REASON tells the change.  The row utils/ledgers/RULE.txt | grow | admit | COMMIT | REASON admits
# the growth that one commit made in a ledger, and nothing more.  REASON tells why the growth is
# wanted.  The row utils/registry.txt | keep | admit | RULE warn|error | REASON keeps a threshold
# above its lowest value, and REASON gives the measurement.  COMMIT is the hash in 9 characters.  A
# row that admits no finding is an error.  `python3 utils/bin/guard explain history` tells the rule.
