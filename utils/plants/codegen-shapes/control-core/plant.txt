# A probe unit with the flags of a Release unit, the hardening flags too, and the same code flags as the foundation
# unit.  The family side of load is its raw side, checked adds the check that its probe declares, and count has the
# loop of its raw side.
part core
unit core_shape_probes test/shape/core_shapes.cpp | -std=c++26 -fcontracts -fno-plt -O3 -march=x86-64-v3 -DNDEBUG -fPIC -fno-strict-overflow -fharden-compares -fharden-conditional-branches -fcontract-evaluation-semantic=observe
unit foundation src/foundation/unit.cpp | -std=c++26 -fcontracts -fno-plt -O3 -march=x86-64-v3 -DNDEBUG -fPIC -fno-strict-overflow -fharden-compares -fharden-conditional-branches -fcontract-evaluation-semantic=observe
probe load | a load is the load of a raw pointer
probe checked check=fatal | a checked load adds a test and a branch to a call of the fatal exit
probe count | a sum over an array is the loop of a raw pointer
