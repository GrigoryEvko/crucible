# The record of one test of the build gives a peak memory of 0.2 GB, under the limit 1.5 GB of the kind test.  A
# second test takes 40 s of wall time and 0.2 GB, and no rule judges its wall time.  judge() of `loop test-step` gives
# no finding.  A small compile and a small link give the module an input.
format 1
kind x86_64-debug-asan
compile CMakeFiles/lib.dir/small.cpp.o src/small.cpp cpu=1 peak=0.1
link test/small_test cpu=1 peak=0.1
test-record test_small wall=1 peak=0.2
test-record test_slow wall=40 peak=0.2
