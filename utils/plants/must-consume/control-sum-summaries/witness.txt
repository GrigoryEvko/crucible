# The summaries of a closure and of a callee (THE SUMMARIES of linear.cpp)
# admit each form of this unit: a callee whose body keeps no reference to the
# sum, a closure that can fill the sum and that the function never calls, a
# closure that fills the sum before its consume, and a closure that only reads
# the sum after its fill or after its consume.  The unit compiles and gives no line of the sums.
compile summaries src/summaries.cpp must-consume=on
flag summaries -freflection
exit summaries pass
