# The summaries of a closure and of a callee (THE SUMMARIES of linear.cpp)
# admit each form of this unit: a callee whose body keeps no reference to the
# sum, also a function template and a callee whose body comes after the
# caller, and a chain of two such callees; a closure that can fill the sum and
# that the function never calls; a closure that fills the sum before its
# consume; a closure that only reads the sum after its fill or after its
# consume, also a generic closure and a closure that a callee takes; a closure
# that consumes the sum itself; and an alias of the sum (THE ALIASES) with no
# call after the consume.  The unit compiles and gives no line of the sums.
compile summaries src/summaries.cpp must-consume=on
flag summaries -freflection
exit summaries pass
