# The rule of the closures that move a capture (THE CLOSURES THAT MOVE A
# CAPTURE of linear.cpp) admits each form of this unit: the once door that
# calls a closure that gives its token out, a closure that only reads its
# linear capture and runs two times, a closure that moves a capture of no
# linear type out and runs two times, a closure that moves its capture out
# and gives it a new value before each exit, a temporary closure that runs one
# time, a door that forwards a closure that the caller gave up, a call of an
# rvalue of a closure that the frame owns, a door that takes the closure by
# value, and a capture of a body that goes to the once door.  The directory
# src/ has the enforce mode error, and the unit compiles with no line of the
# rule.
compile closures src/closures.cpp must-consume=on
flag closures -freflection
exit closures pass
