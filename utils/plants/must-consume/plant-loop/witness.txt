# The fixed point of a loop: the second turn of a loop consumes an outer
# local again (double-consume), and the path of zero turns leaves it with no
# consume (unconsumed-on-path).  A continue leaves a local of the body with no
# consume.  A local of the body that each turn consumes gives no line.

compile loop src/loop.cpp must-consume=on
flag loop -freflection
exit loop pass
judge loop
finding loop must-consume unconsumed-on-path src/loop.cpp:21:11 | planted::outer_local: token
finding loop must-consume double-consume src/loop.cpp:23:13 | planted::outer_local: token
finding loop must-consume unconsumed src/loop.cpp:29:15 | planted::continue_path: turn
