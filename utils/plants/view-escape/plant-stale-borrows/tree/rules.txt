# The rule table of the plant plant-stale-borrows of the rule view-escape.  The
# units of src/ are quarantined, and the planted header of include/foundation/
# is a base file.  The headers of the repository are outside the root of the
# plant, so the table admits the names of the base that the planted code uses.

layer low 0 include/foundation/
allow low <foundation/Transfer.h>

admit foundation::core | the owners, the sums, the Ref and the View whose borrows the planted code reads
admit foundation::AlignedBytes | a block of bytes
admit foundation::alloc | the source of the chunks of a window
admit foundation::effects | the capability of an allocation
admit foundation::permissions | the permission of a lock
admit foundation::brand | the annotation of the family tag of an arena
admit foundation::transfer | the marks of the owner and the borrow of a resource by its number
admit foundation::lifetime | the byte seal of the borrow of a resource by its number
admit fixy | the locks and the regions of the planted code
admit std::move | a cast to an rvalue reference
admit std::construct_at | a construct at a pointer
admit std::as_const | a read through a const reference

quarantine src/
