# Session types and behavioural types, 2022-2026: catalog

This catalog lists the papers that the literature review found. The review, and the design that follows from it, are in `misc/session_types_literature.md`.

The list comes from an OpenAlex citation snowball, OpenCitations queries, arXiv sweeps and papers found by web search. A row marked (read) was read in the PDF. In the other rows, the contribution column is the first sentence of the abstract. It is not a judgement.

The topic filter used keywords. Some rows are not about session types, for example rows about dance choreography or cloud tenancy. The rows stay as the sweep gave them.

The snowball did not get to a fixpoint, because rate limits stopped three services. The true number of papers is larger than the count below.

Author lists in this catalog come from OpenAlex and were not examined one by one. The bibliography in the review document was examined against Crossref and DataCite.

Total unique items from 2022 or later: 301. Source counts before deduplication: arxiv-dropped-non-cs 189, arxiv-dropped-off-topic 209, arxiv-sweep 284, dropped-off-topic-openalex-crawl 7, openalex-crawl 280, read-or-websearch 62.
